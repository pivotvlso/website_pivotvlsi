About PIVOT

At PIVOT, we are dedicated to elevating your potential in the field of VLSI design. We are proudly pioneering the effort to bring TinyTapeout to India, enabling students and professionals to design, tape out, and manufacture their own custom silicon chips.

To further our commitment to excellence in education, new interactive tools are actively being built to deeply enhance the practical understanding of our students.

Our Mission

To democratize VLSI education by providing accessible, premier educational programs, interactive courselabs, and expert video lectures that bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.

Our Vision

To become India's leading platform for semiconductor innovation, empowering the next generation of engineers to turn their silicon dreams into reality and fuel the global VLSI ecosystem.

Meet the Founder

With extensive experience in the semiconductor industry and a passion for education, our founder established PIVOT to bridge the gap between academia and industry. They are dedicated to bringing cutting-edge technologies like TinyTapeout to students across India.
